# Known limitations

Read this before using the data. It is generated from `api.caveats()` (24 flags, each with a severity, the effect on a backtest and how to handle it) plus the open items below. Re-generate it after any rebuild. Last generated 2026-11-24.

**Status of the audit:** audited in-house only (`docs/AUDIT_REPORT.md`, Stages 0-9). The independent review (OPEN_ISSUES A8) has not been supplied, so the project must not be described as production ready or independently audited.

## What the data does not cover

- Membership is verified for 27 of 57 in-scope indices; the other 30 have no historical source and are refused by the API (`api.coverage()`, `api.index_availability()`).
- Adjusted prices are verified from 2007-01-01 only; nothing earlier is claimed.
- Shares outstanding exist only from 2024-02-05; there is no free-float source (NSE publishes none in the files used).
- Adjusted history is flagged `adj_reliable=False` for 97 SME securities and for the named ETFs and others in `data/curated/adjustment_unreliable.csv` (`api.unreliable_adjusted()`).
- 332 index-days where NSE's two index sources disagree by 0.1% or more stay unresolved (NaN returns under `source="rule"`); nothing is chosen by guess.
- About 90 rights and instrument events since 2007 carry no usable size (OPEN_ISSUES A20); Tata Steel 2007 differs from Yahoo by about 22% for 205 days.
- Raw files under `data/raw/market/` (about 2.9 GB) are kept in Google Drive, not git. A fresh clone fails `verify`'s manifest check until they are restored.

## Caveat flags

| Severity | Flag | Category | What | Effect on a backtest | How to handle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH | `SME_ADJUSTED_PRICES` | PRICES | SME-platform securities (series SM/ST): the NSE corporate-action feed holds no records for them, so none of their splits or bonuses is applied. | Adjusted series carry unadjusted drops (a 2:1 bonus is a -50% day). Momentum, volatility, drawdown and stop rules see crashes that never happened; a stock can be dropped or bought on them. | Filter on adj_reliable, or drop api.sme_securities() from the universe, or use raw prices (exact). |
| HIGH | `CURATED_UNRELIABLE_ADJUSTED` | PRICES | Known wrong adjusted history on named securities: ETFs with non-round unit ratios, a real bonus the price test rejected (SUNTV), CCAVENUE's unrecorded 10:1 consolidation. | Same as SME: a fake jump or crash on the day, and every earlier adjusted price is off by the missing factor. | Filter on adj_reliable or exclude api.unreliable_adjusted().sec_id. |
| HIGH | `ADJUSTED_BEFORE_2007` | PRICES | Adjusted prices before 2007-01-01 are not verified: the feed holds almost no splits or bonuses for those years. | Backtests starting before 2007 would use unadjusted history for older actions. | The API refuses adjusted prices before 2007-01-01 unless allow_unverified=True (then treat as unverified). |
| HIGH | `MEMBERSHIP_NOT_VERIFIED` | MEMBERSHIP | Index membership is verified only on the windows in coverage(); other dates and the roughly 30 indices without historical sheets are refused. | No member list exists for those dates; any estimate would be a model. | Query within api.coverage() windows. The API raises CoverageError otherwise. |
| HIGH | `MEMBERSHIP_BACKFILLED` | MEMBERSHIP | index_members(..., backfill=True) returns, for dates from 2007-01-01 up to an index's first verified day, the first verified list restricted to securities already trading. | Survivorship and look-ahead bias: members are chosen with knowledge of later outcomes. Not suitable for out-of-sample claims. | Use only as a stress test; prefer Nifty 50/Next 50/Nifty 100, verified from 2007. |
| MEDIUM | `FEED_ENDS` | ACTIONS | The corporate-action feed is a download as of a date (see the manifest); actions announced after it are missing and appear as unexplained jumps. | Recent splits and bonuses are not adjusted until the feed is refreshed. | Refresh the feed after each month-end (A19) and rebuild; treat the last weeks as unadjusted. |
| MEDIUM | `UNQUANTIFIED_CORPORATE_ACTIONS` | ACTIONS | Events the feed gives no usable size for: rights issues with no issue price, text the parser cannot read (about 90 UNPARSED/UNKNOWN since 2007), and about 600 mergers, demergers, buy-backs and capital reductions: recorded, not quantified. | No adjustment is applied. For rights this shifts earlier adjusted prices by the missing dilution (typically a few percent to 20%); total_return mode misses an unparsed dividend. | Treat the security's returns around the ex-date as unreliable; list the events with api.explain(). |
| MEDIUM | `DOCUMENTED_ADJUSTMENT_BREAK` | PRICES | Demergers and schemes the feed gives no ratio for (about 108), plus overnight moves nothing explains. The break stays in the adjusted series. | One day of fake return (typically -30% to -70%) on the day of the event; cumulative adjusted returns before it are overstated or understated by that move. | api.prices(..., mask_gaps=True) sets adj_return to NaN on those days; or skip the security around the date. |
| MEDIUM | `INDEX_VALUE_CONFLICTS` | INDEX | NSE's two index sources disagree on about 1,061 index-days (338 by 0.1% or more) and about 950 index-days are missing inside index lives. | A return computed across a conflicting or missing day is unreliable. | index_values(..., mask_conflicts=True); both values are kept (alt_close). |
| MEDIUM | `FEED_LAGS_BHAVCOPY` | ACTIONS | The newest corporate-action feed file ends more than 14 days before the newest bhavcopy. The feed cannot be downloaded by script, so it ages between manual downloads; `update` prints a warning when this happens. | Splits, bonuses and dividends after the feed end are not applied; a split shows as a one-day crash in adjusted prices. | Download the feed in a browser (NSE Corporate Actions, Equity), save it in data/raw/corporate_actions, rebuild; until then treat the weeks after the feed end as unadjusted. |
| MEDIUM | `MEMBERSHIP_2007_2009_THIN_EVIDENCE` | MEMBERSHIP | 2007-05 to 2009-12 rests on one archived list (Nifty 50, 2007-04-30), the workbook IndexInclExcl.xls (origin unverified) and press releases; the daily size and subset checks pass. | Lower evidence than later years; a compensating pair of errors would not be caught. | Back-test from 2010 for fully confirmed membership. |
| MEDIUM | `SEC_ID_NOT_STABLE` | GENERAL | sec_id labels are renumbered when the data is rebuilt. | Anything saved by sec_id can point at another security after a rebuild. | Key durable data by symbol plus ISIN. |
| MEDIUM | `DATA_NOT_IN_THE_BUILD` | MARKET | Not in the data: free float, and shares outstanding / market cap before 2024-02-05. From 2024-02-05 api.shares_outstanding() gives NSE's issued share count per day (market cap = raw close x that count, computed by you, not float-adjusted). | Rules that need market cap or float before 2024-02, or any float-based rule, cannot be tested; equal-weight and index-level work are unaffected. | Use api.shares_outstanding() for 2024-02-05 onward only; see OPEN_ISSUES A21 for the niftyindices weight files. |
| MEDIUM | `IMPACT_COST` | MARKET | NSE publishes Security Category Impact Cost (C_CATG_<MMM><YYYY>.T01) monthly, not daily: 6-month rolling mean impact cost (%) for a standard Rs 1 lakh order (no order-size column in the file; fixed by NSE Clearing methodology) plus liquidity Group 1/2/3. Continuous on nsearchives from 2023-10 onward (plus 9 earlier months: 2019-03, 2021-01..06, 2023-05..06). Blank for Group 3, debt and illiquid series; never filled. | Cannot measure daily or order-size-specific slippage; pre-2023-10 history is mostly absent. | Use api.impact_cost() as a monthly liquidity/cost screen for Rs 1 lakh orders from 2023-10 onward; treat missing values as unreported/illiquid, never zero. |
| MEDIUM | `PRICE_BAND_BEFORE_2020` | MARKET | Circuit limits are exact from 2020 (daily security list). Before that only the band changes since 2010 are known: the band is the last recorded change, and a security with no change recorded is refused. | A rule on circuit limits cannot be tested before 2020 for stocks that never changed band. | price_band() raises CoverageError when the band is unknown. |
| MEDIUM | `INDEX_HISTORY_IS_BACKCAST` | INDEX | NSE's published index levels before an index's launch date are back-calculated by NSE (for example Nifty Midcap 150 from 2005-04-01). | The level series exists, but the constituents behind it were never published, so it cannot be reproduced from membership. | Use the levels as benchmarks, not as evidence of what the index held. |
| LOW | `DUAL_CLASS_EXTRA_MEMBER` | MEMBERSHIP | Tata Motors DVR was an extra security in several indices (51 securities for 50 companies) while the parent was a member. | Member counts exceed the official size by one on those days; weights are not in the data. | Count companies, not securities, if a rule depends on the index size. |
| LOW | `PARTIAL_WEEKEND_SESSIONS` | MARKET | Two weekend special sessions, 2010-05-16 and 2012-11-11, are in the trading calendar with partial bhavcopies (7 and 14 symbols) and no index file. | A rule that assumes a full cross-section on every trading day breaks on those two days; index members look untraded and index levels are missing. | Skip 2010-05-16 and 2012-11-11, or use only days with a full cross-section. |
| LOW | `T0_CLOSE_OUTSIDE_RANGE` | PRICES | On 217 of 241 rows of series T0 (trade-to-trade, one-time segment) the close lies outside the day's low-high range. No other series has this. | A check that close is within [low, high] fails on T0 rows. | Exclude series T0 from such checks, or from the universe. |
| LOW | `EX_DATE_NOT_ANNOUNCEMENT_DATE` | ACTIONS | NSE's feed carries no announcement dates; adjustments use ex-dates. This is point-in-time safe for prices, but 'what was known when' is not available. | as_of= limits actions by ex-date, not by announcement date; a strategy that reacts to announcements cannot be tested. | Do not use the feed to time announcement-driven signals. |
| LOW | `DIVIDENDS_FROM_FEED_TEXT` | ACTIONS | Cash dividends come from the feed's free text; ambiguous forms (e.g. 'Rs .50', 'Rs.0125') are left out, and a dividend with no trade within 7 days is not applied. | total_return mode can miss a dividend; price_return mode is unaffected. | Use adjust_mode='splits' for price returns; treat total_return as approximate. |
| LOW | `DELIVERY_SOURCE_DISAGREEMENTS` | MARKET | NSE's delivery file and the bhavcopy disagree on traded quantity for about 0.07% of rows (for example EIDPARRY 2004-01-02). | A delivery percentage on those rows rests on one of two conflicting volumes. | Compare api.delivery().traded_qty with the bhavcopy volume when a delivery signal triggers on a single day. |
| LOW | `FO_ELIGIBILITY_IS_DERIVED` | MARKET | F&O eligibility is taken from NSE's derivatives bhavcopy: a stock counts as eligible on a day if it has stock futures or options contracts that day. NSE's official eligible list is not in the data. | A stock newly admitted or about to be removed can differ by days from the official list; stocks with contracts only in far months still count. | Use api.fo_eligible(date) as 'has contracts that day'; do not read it as the official list. |
| LOW | `DEALS_REVISED_BY_EXCHANGE` | MARKET | NSE revises historical bulk/block/short-selling deal records on members' request (stated on NSE's report page), so a later download can differ from an earlier one. | Backtests using deal signals cannot assume the published history is immutable. | Treat historical deal records as snapshot-dated by manifest fetched_utc; refresh if exact current exchange records are needed. |
