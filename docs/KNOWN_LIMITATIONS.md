# Known limitations

Generated from `api.caveats()` (14 flags) on 2026-11-24 after the data bank was cut to its core. Re-generate after a rebuild.

**Audit status:** audited in-house only (`docs/AUDIT_REPORT.md`). The independent review (OPEN_ISSUES A8) has not been supplied; do not describe the data as independently audited.

## What the bank does not contain

- Membership before 2010-01-01 (except opt-in backfill) and for any index outside the 27 verified equity indices.
- Adjusted prices before 2007-01-01; dividends and total return.
- Adjusted series for SME securities and the securities in `api.unreliable_adjusted()`. Other ETFs cannot be identified in the data.
- Price bands, ASM/GSM, F&O lists, deals, impact cost, market cap, shares outstanding, free float.
- Unquantified rights and demergers stay unadjusted and are masked on their day.
- The bhavcopy and delivery raw files live in Google Drive, not git.

## Caveat flags

| Severity | Flag | Category | What | Effect on a backtest | How to handle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HIGH | `MEMBERSHIP_NOT_VERIFIED` | MEMBERSHIP | Index membership is verified only on the windows in coverage() (from 2010-01-01, or from the index's launch if later); other dates and indices outside the 27 verified equity indices are refused. | No member list exists for those dates; any estimate would be a model. | Query within api.coverage() windows. The API raises CoverageError otherwise. |
| HIGH | `MEMBERSHIP_BACKFILLED` | MEMBERSHIP | index_members(..., backfill=True) returns, for dates from 2007-01-01 up to an index's first verified day, the first verified list restricted to securities already trading. | Survivorship and look-ahead bias: members are chosen with knowledge of later outcomes. Not suitable for out-of-sample claims. | Opt-in only. Use it as a stress test or for a proxy universe, not as the published membership. |
| HIGH | `MEMBERSHIP_RECONSTRUCTED` | MEMBERSHIP | index_members(..., reconstruct=True) returns a rule-based rebuild (rank by 6-month average market capitalisation, current methodology bands) for unverified dates from 2007-04, for 16 size-rank indices; sectoral indices are refused. Shares are 2024-26 issued capital carried back through splits and bonuses (imputed from traded value where none), no free float, no F&O/turnover screens or buffers. | A model, not NSE's list: boundary members are often wrong, delisted stocks are sized from traded value, today's share counts leak into the past. | Opt-in only; read api.reconstruction_accuracy() for the index and era. Mean Jaccard vs the verified lists: Nifty 500 0.82, LargeMidcap 250 0.89, Midcap 150 0.75, Nifty 100 0.76, Nifty 50 0.59, Next 50 0.40, Midcap 50 0.28. |
| MEDIUM | `DOCUMENTED_ADJUSTMENT_BREAK` | PRICES | Demergers and schemes the feed gives no ratio for (about 108), plus overnight moves nothing explains. The break stays in the adjusted series. | One day of fake return (typically -30% to -70%) on the day of the event; cumulative adjusted prices before it are overstated or understated by that move. | prices() sets adj_return to NaN on documented-event days (class ON_ACTION_DAY) by default (mask_gaps='events'); UNEXPLAINED moves are flagged but not masked because they can be real crashes (mask_gaps='all' masks them too). The days are in frame.attrs['flagged'] and api.adjustment_gaps(). Do not compound adj_close across a flagged day. |
| MEDIUM | `DEMERGER_ESTIMATED_FACTOR` | PRICES | Five demergers carry an ESTIMATED adjustment factor (data/curated/demerger_estimates.csv: Bajaj Holdings 2008, Alembic 2011, Triveni 2011, ABFRL 2025, SKF India 2025): the documented 1:1 ratio, valued at the first listing day of the new company as parent close / (parent close + new close). Accepted only where it agrees with the parent's own ex-day move within 10% (it differed by -6% to +3%); three other 1:1 demergers (Zuari, Orient Paper, Raymond) disagreed by 22-53% and stay unadjusted. | Adjusted prices before the event are scaled by an estimate, accurate to a few percent, not exact. The event day's own return carries that error. | The event day is class DEMERGER_ESTIMATED and its adj_return is NaN by default (mask_gaps='events'). Use adjust_mode=None if you need exact prices. |
| MEDIUM | `UNQUANTIFIED_CORPORATE_ACTIONS` | ACTIONS | Events the feed gives no usable size for: rights issues with no issue price, text the parser cannot read (about 90 UNPARSED/UNKNOWN since 2007), and about 600 mergers, demergers, buy-backs and capital reductions: recorded, not quantified. | No adjustment is applied. For rights this shifts earlier adjusted prices by the missing dilution (typically a few percent to 20%). | Treat the security's returns around the ex-date as unreliable; list the events with api.explain(). |
| MEDIUM | `NOT_SERVED_ADJUSTED` | PRICES | Adjusted prices are refused for SME-platform securities (the feed holds no records for them) and for the named securities in data/curated/adjustment_unreliable.csv (ETFs with non-round unit ratios, SUNTV, CCAVENUE, GULFPETRO, AHLEAST). ETFs are not otherwise identifiable in the data: other ETFs trade in series EQ and are adjusted like any stock. | prices() raises CoverageError for them with an adjust_mode; as-published prices (adjust_mode=None) are exact. | Build the equity universe from api.securities() and drop api.sme_securities() and api.unreliable_adjusted(); ETFs have to be excluded by your own list. |
| MEDIUM | `FEED_ENDS` | ACTIONS | The corporate-action feed is a download as of a date (see the manifest); actions announced after it are missing and appear as unexplained jumps. | Recent splits and bonuses are not adjusted until the feed is refreshed. | Refresh the feed after each month-end (A19) and rebuild; treat the last weeks as unadjusted. |
| MEDIUM | `FEED_LAGS_BHAVCOPY` | ACTIONS | The newest corporate-action feed file ends more than 14 days before the newest bhavcopy. The feed cannot be downloaded by script, so it ages between manual downloads; `update` prints a warning when this happens. | Splits and bonuses after the feed end are not applied; a split shows as a one-day crash in adjusted prices. | Download the feed in a browser (NSE Corporate Actions, Equity), save it in data/raw/corporate_actions, rebuild; until then treat the weeks after the feed end as unadjusted. |
| MEDIUM | `INDEX_HISTORY_IS_BACKCAST` | INDEX | NSE's published index levels before an index's launch date are back-calculated by NSE (for example Nifty Midcap 150 from 2005-04-01). | The level series exists, but the constituents behind it were never published, so it cannot be reproduced from membership. | Use the levels as benchmarks, not as evidence of what the index held. |
| MEDIUM | `SEC_ID_NOT_STABLE` | GENERAL | sec_id labels are renumbered when the data is rebuilt. | Anything saved by sec_id can point at another security after a rebuild. | Key durable data by symbol plus ISIN. |
| LOW | `EX_DATE_NOT_ANNOUNCEMENT_DATE` | ACTIONS | NSE's feed carries no announcement dates; adjustments use ex-dates. This is point-in-time safe for prices, but 'what was known when' is not available. | as_of= limits actions by ex-date, not by announcement date; a strategy that reacts to announcements cannot be tested. | Do not use the feed to time announcement-driven signals. |
| LOW | `PARTIAL_WEEKEND_SESSIONS` | MARKET | Two weekend special sessions, 2010-05-16 and 2012-11-11, are in the trading calendar with partial bhavcopies (7 and 14 symbols) and no index file. | A rule that assumes a full cross-section on every trading day breaks on those two days; index members look untraded and index levels are missing. | Skip 2010-05-16 and 2012-11-11, or use only days with a full cross-section. |
| LOW | `T0_CLOSE_OUTSIDE_RANGE` | PRICES | On 217 of 241 rows of series T0 (trade-to-trade, one-time segment) the close lies outside the day's low-high range. No other series has this. | A check that close is within [low, high] fails on T0 rows. | Exclude series T0 from such checks, or from the universe. |
| LOW | `DUAL_CLASS_EXTRA_MEMBER` | MEMBERSHIP | Tata Motors DVR was an extra security in several indices (51 securities for 50 companies) while the parent was a member. | Member counts exceed the official size by one on those days; weights are not in the data. | Count companies, not securities, if a rule depends on the index size. |
| LOW | `DELIVERY_SOURCE_DISAGREEMENTS` | MARKET | NSE's delivery file and the bhavcopy disagree on traded quantity for about 0.07% of rows. | A delivery percentage on those rows rests on one of two conflicting volumes. | Compare api.delivery().traded_qty with the bhavcopy volume when a delivery signal triggers on a single day. |
